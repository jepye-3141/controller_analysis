# Plan J3 — Inverse Launch-Parameter Targeting as the Journal Delta

**Goal:** supply the substantial-new-content requirement for a journal version by promoting the in-repo targeting pipeline (LHS lookup → GP surrogates → SQP inverse optimization) from "future work" (root.tex §Future Work) to a full contribution: *given an operational target, choose the launch parameters — and the deployment point — that maximize reachable, accurate landing.*
**Status:** proposal. Builds directly on `2026-07-09_implementation_strategies_launch_parameter_targeting.md` (Strategies A/E/C + step zero) and the companion literature review; this plan selects, sequences, and adds the paper-integration work.
**Positioning:** turns the paper from "a saturation-handled controller" into "a deployment-planning capability with a certified inner loop" — the CARP-analog framing from the lit review, suitable for JGCD or JAIS.

## Why this blocks acceptance

- The existing pipeline is not citable today: `logs/centroid_lookup_log.mat` (2026-05-09) and `logs/surrogate_optimize_log.mat` (2026-05-12) predate both the `ballistic_success` criterion and the 2026-07-07 `eom2` fix — every stored ratio, centroid, and GP fit is inconsistent with the paper's current numbers.
- Known weaknesses to fix before review *(counts corrected 2026-07-10 against the actual saved log; status annotated after the same-day W0 execution)*: the May-9 log holds N=59 LHS samples (not the script-default 20) over a 6-D box whose `p ∈ [−2,2]` range **excludes the operating spin −8.379** *(fixed: box now [−12, 12], log regenerated)*; the `half_radius = NaN` clamp distorts **52/59** training samples (88%, and it conflates "wider than grid" with "nothing landed") *(fixed: fresh data has 8/41 NaN, all wide, and the area-proxy refit removes the imputation entirely)*; `gp_reach` fit but unused in J(θ) *(examined: report-only now data-defensible — see W1.3)*; λ=50 unexamined *(examined: front flat past λ ≈ 10)*; `p_target(3)` silently ignored *(still open — also true of `j3_surrogate_refit.m`; document or fix at promotion)*; deploy-at-apogee never relaxed *(open — W2)*.

### W2 — Deployment point as a decision variable (Strategy E; highest insight per day)
Parameterize deploy-state extraction as `t_deploy = t_apogee + Δt` (bounded, tight range first) in the wrapper between `eom2` and `sweep_landing_centroid`; add Δt as the 7th dimension of the LHS/optimization. ### W3 — Target-weighted reachability objective (Strategy C; optional, needs an operational input)
